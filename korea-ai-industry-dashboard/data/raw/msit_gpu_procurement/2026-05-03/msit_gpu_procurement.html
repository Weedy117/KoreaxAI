GPU Procurement Project (First Supplementary Budget, KRW 1.46 Trillion) Selects Participants

담당부서
작성자
연락처

- Competition among applicants and vendors and active negotiations leads to a plan to secure 13,000 cutting-edge GPUs, significantly exceeding the original plan
- Partner companies actively join efforts to build a thriving sovereign AI ecosystem in Korea
- Phased distribution to begin by the end of the year following rapid procurement of GPUs

​

The Ministry of Science and ICT (“MSIT”; Minister Bae Kyunghoon) has announced the selection of Naver Cloud, NHN Cloud, and Kakao as partners for a large-scale graphics processing units (GPUs) Procurement Project (First Supplementary Budget, KRW 1.46 trillion). In partnership with these companies, the MSIT will secure 13,000 advanced GPUs.

​

This initiative is expected to serve as an important starting point for a quantum leap in domestic GPU research and service development, and to revitalize the AI ecosystem by swiftly expanding domestic AI computing infrastructure and providing timely support to industry, academia, and research institutions in need of such resources.

​

【 Overview 】

​

The Korean government allocated a supplementary budget of approximately KRW 1.46 trillion in May for the procurement of advanced GPUs. Following this, it issued a call for participation (May 23-June 23), conducted evaluations of proposals, carried out on-site inspections of data centers, and reviewed and coordinated project budgets. This came with the recognition that Korea’s AI ecosystem requires the maximum possible availability of GPU resources and stable service provision by companies with proven expertise and capabilities. To this end, the government engaged in close negotiations and active collaboration with stakeholders, leading to the selection of Naver Cloud, NHN Cloud, and Kakao as the project’s implementing partners.

​

【 GPU Procurement 】

​

Through close public-private collaboration, the initiative will secure a total of 13,000 GPUs, comprising 10,080 B200 units and 3,056 H200 units.

* In terms of Floating Point (FP) 8 Tensor Core performance, the B200 delivers approximately 2.25 times the computational capacity of the H200.

​

A portion of this will be allocated for the partnering Cloud Service Providers’ (CSPs) for their own operational needs. More than 10,000 GPUs ― specifically, 8,160 B200 units and 2,296 H200 units ― will be available for government-led use.

​

The government’s GPU allocation will be organized into one B200 510-node cluster (4,080 units), two B200 255-node clusters (2,040 units each), and one H200 255-node cluster (2,040 units), enabling support for large-scale, cluster-based AI workloads.

* GPU clustering links multiple GPUs to function as a single, integrated computing system.

​

【 GPU Allocation by CSP 】

​

The allocation of GPUs to be secured and deployed by each CSP is as follows:

​

Naver Cloud - Naver Cloud will secure and deploy a total of 3,056 H200 units, configured entirely with the H200 (Hopper architecture), the current mainstream model on the market, ensuring stable GPU services within the year.

​

Of these, 2,296 H200 units will be allocated for government use. These will be clustered into one 255-node (2,040 units) and one 32-node (256 units) and will be distributed to industry, academia, and research institutions starting this year.

​

NHN Cloud - NHN Cloud will secure and deploy a total of 7,656 B200 units, configured entirely with the B200 (Blackwell architecture) and operated using a liquid cooling system. This configuration is expected to enable the latest GPU clusters to run with superior performance, energy efficiency, and environmental sustainability.

* Liquid cooling offers higher energy efficiency and superior cooling performance compared to air cooling, enhancing GPU performance.

​

Of these, 6,120 B200 units will be allocated for government use. They will be clustered into one B200 510-node (4,080 units) and one B200 255-node (2,040 units). Some resources will be made available in beta service within the year.

* Clustering will begin with the 255-node in Q1 2026, followed by the completion of the 510-node cluster later in 2026.​

​

Kakao - Kakao will secure and deploy a total of 2,424 B200 units, configured entirely with the B200, with a focus on operating a more efficient infrastructure.

​

Of these, 2,040 B200 units will be allocated for government use and clustered into one B200 255-node (2,040 units). Some of these resources will also be made available in beta service within the year.

​

【 Partner Companies’ Plans for Domestic AI Ecosystem Development 】

​

All partering companies have expressed strong commitment to advancing Korea’s sovereign AI ecosystem and to working closely with the government to maximize the availability of GPUs for public allocation. They also plan to utilize their own GPU allocations―taking into account operational cost requirements―to drive their own AI development and capability enhancement, while offering GPUs at low cost to industry, academia, and research institutes. In addition, some partners intend to increase investment in domestic AI infrastructure, thereby contributing to the ecosystem in multiple ways.

​

The partnering companies will also collaborate to build a tentatively named Online Platform for Integrated GPU Support. This platform will allow stakeholders from industry, academia, and research institutions to easily apply for GPU resources online, with allocation and distribution to follow an evaluation process.

* The platform will include a system to allocate idle GPU resources to standby users when they become available.

​

【 Going Forward 】

​

Going forward, the government plans to swiftly proceed with business agreements and GPU procurement orders as early as the beginning of August, following a series of procedures. It also plans to initiate phased GPU distribution** for national projects* and necessary industry-academia-research collaborations.

* e.g. Independent AI Foundation Model Project / ** Phased distribution of GPUs will start this year

​

This will facilitate 1) stronger domestic AI research and service development capabilities; 2) enhanced access to AI computing infrastructure for early-stage AI startups, small and medium-sized enterprises, and universities; and 3) faster development of large-scale AI models.

​

Minister Bae Kyunghoon of the MSIT stated, “The acquisition of these advanced GPUs serves as a catalyst for addressing the shortage of AI computing infrastructure in Korea, and marks the starting point for the new administration’s expansion of the sovereign AI ecosystem and the construction of an AI highway, which will accelerate innovation across the entire domestic AI ecosystem. Taking this as a starting point, we will devote our policy efforts to further strengthening AI computing infrastructure and propelling Korea into becoming a global AI leader.”​

​

For further information, please contact the Public Relations Division (Phone: +82-44-202-4034, E-mail: msitmedia@korea.kr) of the Ministry of Science and ICT.
