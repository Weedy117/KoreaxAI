# Limitations

- v0.1 is a first usable snapshot, not a complete South Korea AI country profile.
- Private AI compute is not comprehensively observable. TOP500, Epoch AI, company announcements, and official procurement targets each cover different parts of the compute picture.
- Some market reports, PDFs, data portals, and government statistics require manual table extraction or category mapping.
- Official targets are not deployed capacity.
- Company announcements are not audited installed capacity.
- Survey metrics depend on field dates, question wording, and sampling methodology.
- OpenAlex publication counts depend on the selected AI concept filter and country-affiliation rule.
- Stanford AI Index local public-data files may not include South Korea for every country-year chart.
- OpenDART extraction requires an API key and careful filing taxonomy work, which is intentionally outside the v0.1 parser.
